This concentration in a few sectors explains the divergence seen in the A-D line—while major tech stocks surge, many smaller or less favored stocks may be lagging or declining. The robust retail investor activity and bullish sentiment fueled by strong AI chip demand support the high percentages above moving averages and signal continued interest in growth sectors.

For the coming weeks, these conditions suggest a market environment where leadership by a handful of key sectors might persist, potentially resulting in mixed breadth readings.
